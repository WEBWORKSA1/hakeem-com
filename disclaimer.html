Medical disclaimer

Please read this before acting on anything you find on Hakeem.com.

The content on Hakeem.com, including herb profiles, guides, tools, the interaction checker, the mizaj quiz and videos, is provided for general education. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it does not create a relationship between you and Hakeem.com or any practitioner.

  • Always seek the advice of your doctor, pharmacist or another qualified health professional about a medical condition, and before starting, stopping or changing any medicine, herb or supplement.
  • Never stop taking a prescribed medicine because of something you read here.
  • Never delay seeking medical care because of information on this site.
  • If you think you have a medical emergency, call your local emergency number immediately.

Traditional use

Descriptions of traditional use, including Unani temperament (mizaj) classifications, describe historical practice. They are not claims that a remedy is effective or safe.

Interaction checker

The checker covers a selection of well-documented interactions. It is not complete, and the absence of a warning does not mean a combination is safe.

Practitioner introductions

Hakeem.com may introduce you to independent practitioners. We check registration where a profession is regulated, but we do not supervise their care and are not responsible for their advice or treatment. Agree fees and treatment plans directly with them, and keep your own doctor informed.

Products

Herbal products vary widely in strength and purity. Information on this site about a herb does not apply to any particular brand or product.

Reporting side effects

If you think a herbal product has harmed you, tell your doctor or pharmacist and report it to your national regulator, for example MedWatch in the United States, MedEffect in Canada, the Yellow Card scheme in the United Kingdom, or the Pharmacovigilance Programme for ASU&H drugs in India.
