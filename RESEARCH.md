## Features adopted on Hakeem.com

1. **Herb monograph template**: names in Urdu/Arabic/Hindi, Latin name and family, quick facts, a three-line summary, an "Our take" verdict, evidence by use (graded), traditional use by system (labelled as tradition), what research shows, safety and who should avoid, interactions, "don't confuse with", buying tips, references, reviewed date, practitioner CTA.
2. **Five-level evidence scale** (Strong, Moderate, Limited, Insufficient, Not supported) with a public method page.
3. **Temperament (mizaj) map** plotting herbs on hot↔cold / dry↔moist axes — the site's signature visual.
4. **Herb–drug interaction checker** covering 18 medicine classes and situations.
5. **Mizaj quiz** — full result on screen, optional email, separate unticked consents, red-flag messaging.
6. **Concern hubs** (digestion, heart, blood sugar, mood and sleep, pain, skin and mouth, women's health, colds) for internal linking.
7. **Glossary** of Unani terms.
8. **Lead funnel**: three-step consultation request + four-step practitioner listing application with Free / Verified / Featured plans.
9. **Support**: one-time/monthly toggle, presets with impact lines, fund allocation, supporter tiers, sponsorship tiers, Adopt-an-Herb.
10. **Contests**: tiered prizes, countdown, judging criteria, official rules, no purchase necessary.
11. **Trust pages**: about, editorial policy, evidence method, corrections, advertising policy, medical disclaimer, privacy, cookies, terms, trademark/copyright disclosure.
12. **AdSense layout**: Auto ads; no ads on 404; ad loader kept off the three conversion pages (consultation request, practitioner listing, support) so ads never compete with forms; ads never inside safety boxes.
