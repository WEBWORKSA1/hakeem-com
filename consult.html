What happens next

  1. We read your request and check which practitioners in our network fit your tradition, location and format.
  2. We email you with next steps, usually within one working day, and introduce you to up to three practitioners where available.
  3. You choose whether to book. Fees are agreed directly with the practitioner.

Our standards for practitioners

  • Where a country regulates the profession, practitioners must show current registration, for example with India's National Commission for Indian System of Medicine or Pakistan's National Council for Tibb.
  • They agree never to advise stopping prescribed medicines and never to claim cures.
  • Paid placements are always labelled as sponsored.

Not for emergencies

If you have chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, thoughts of harming yourself or any other emergency, call your local emergency number now.
