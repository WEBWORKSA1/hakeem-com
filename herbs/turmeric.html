  • Turmeric used in food is considered safe.
  • Supplements can cause stomach upset, nausea and diarrhoea.
  • Rare but serious liver injury has been reported with turmeric and curcumin supplements, especially high-absorption products.
  • May worsen gallstones or bile-duct problems.
  • Some imported turmeric powders have been found adulterated with lead chromate to brighten the colour.
