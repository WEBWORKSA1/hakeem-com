Home / Support

Keep good herbal information free

Hakeem.com has no paywall and does not sell supplements. Reader support pays for the slow work: checking every claim against its source, updating safety notes, translating, and reaching people who would otherwise rely on rumour.

Choose your support

We send a secure payment link by email; we never ask for card details on this page. Hakeem.com is not a registered charity, so contributions are not tax-deductible.

Where support goes

Our planned allocation of reader support for the year ahead:

Running costs 30%
Source checks and safety reviews 25%
Hiring reviewers, writers and translators 20%
Promotion and outreach 15%
Contests and prizes 10%

We publish a yearly summary of what came in and where it went.

Monthly supporter tiers

  • Seed, $5 a month. Supporters wall and a monthly note on what your support made possible.
  • Sprout, $15 a month. Plus early access to contests and a vote on which herbs we review next.
  • Grove, $50 a month. Plus a "supported by" credit on one herb profile and a yearly call with the editors.
