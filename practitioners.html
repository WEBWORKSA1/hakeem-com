Why list with Hakeem.com

Who we accept

Practitioners with a recognised qualification in Unani, Ayurveda, Chinese medicine, Western herbalism or naturopathy, or licensed doctors and pharmacists who use herbal medicine. Where your country regulates your profession, current registration is required.
