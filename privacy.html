Privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Hakeem.com ("we", "us") is an educational website about herbal and traditional medicine. You can reach us about privacy through our contact form.

What we collect

  • Information you give us in forms: for example your name, email, phone number, city, the type of help you want, and anything you choose to write in a message. Consultation requests may include general health information such as the concern you want help with.
  • Information collected automatically by our hosting provider and advertising partner: device and browser type, IP address, pages viewed and referring site.
  • Information stored in your browser: your theme and cookie choices, saved in local storage.

We do not store your mizaj quiz answers or interaction-checker selections; they stay in your browser unless you choose to email your result.

How we use it

  • To reply to your message or request, and to introduce you to practitioners when you ask us to.
  • To send the newsletter if you subscribe; every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To run contests, process listing applications and recruitment.
  • To keep the site secure and understand how it is used.
  • To show advertising (see below).

Sharing

We share a consultation request only with practitioners you have agreed to be introduced to, and only the details needed for them to contact you. Form submissions are delivered to us through a form-processing service. We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through a Google-certified consent message before personalised ads are shown.

Embedded videos

Videos are loaded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode, and only after you press play.

How long we keep information

We keep messages and requests for up to 24 months, job applications for 12 months and contest entries for 24 months after the contest ends, unless the law requires longer.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Canada's PIPEDA and Quebec's Law 25, and California law, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Contact us to make a request; we respond within 30 days.

Children

Hakeem.com is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contest entrants under 18 need a parent's or guardian's permission.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. No method of transmission is perfectly secure, so please do not send detailed medical records through our forms.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
