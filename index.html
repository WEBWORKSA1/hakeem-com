Hakeem.com checks the herbs of Unani, Ayurveda and other healing traditions against modern research, so you can see what works, what is unproven, and what is unsafe with your medicines.

Every herb has a temperament. In Unani medicine each remedy is classed as hot or cold, dry or moist. Tap a dot to see the herb and how strong the modern evidence is.

Positions are approximate. Classical texts differ, and temperament describes tradition, not proof that a herb works.

How we grade evidence

Tradition tells us how a plant has been used. Evidence tells us whether it works. We show both, and never mix them up.

Read our method

Strong

Consistent results from several good-quality human trials or official approval for this use.

Moderate

Several human trials point the same way, but they are small, short or uneven in quality.

Limited

A few small human studies, mixed results, or mostly lab and animal data.

Insufficient

Too little reliable human research to say whether it works.

A fifth label, Not supported, marks uses where good trials found no benefit, such as ginkgo for preventing dementia.
