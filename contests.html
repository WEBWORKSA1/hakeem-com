Photography

Medicinal plant photo contest

Photograph a medicinal plant growing in your garden, market or region: kalonji in flower, a neem tree, saffron at harvest. Your own original photos only.

Prizes: Grand prize US$250. Three first prizes of US$100. Ten runners-up featured on the site.

Judged on photographic quality (40%), botanical accuracy (30%) and story (30%).

Writing

Family remedy story contest

Tell the story of a remedy your family used, who taught it and what it meant. Up to 800 words in English, Urdu or Hindi. Stories, not medical claims.

Prizes: Grand prize US$150. Two runners-up of US$50. Winners published with credit.

Judged on storytelling (40%), cultural insight (40%) and clarity (20%).

Quiz

Monthly herb quiz

Twenty questions on herbs, safety and Unani history. Everyone scoring 18 or more enters the monthly draw.

Prize: A US$50 gift card each month, drawn at random from top scorers.

Quiz link is emailed on the first of each month to entrants and newsletter subscribers.

Official rules (summary)

  1. No purchase necessary. Entry is free. A purchase or donation does not improve your chances.
  2. Eligibility. Open worldwide to individuals, except where prohibited by law. Entrants under 18 need a parent's or guardian's permission. Hakeem.com staff and judges cannot win.
  3. Season. Each season ends on the last day of the calendar quarter at 23:59 UTC, shown at the top of this page.
  4. Entries. Up to three entries per person per contest. Entries must be original, unpublished elsewhere for prize contests, and free of medical claims.
  5. Judging. A panel of at least two Hakeem.com editors scores entries against the published criteria. Quiz prizes are drawn at random from qualifying scores.
  6. Prizes. Cash prizes are paid by bank transfer, PayPal or UPI within 30 days of announcement. Winners are responsible for any taxes. A skill-testing question may be required where local law requires one.
  7. Rights. You keep copyright. You grant Hakeem.com a non-exclusive licence to publish your entry with credit on the site, newsletter and social channels.
  8. Privacy. We use your details only to run the contest, as set out in our privacy policy.
  9. Changes. We may modify or cancel a contest if it cannot run as planned, and will tell entrants by email.
