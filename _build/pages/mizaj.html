What is your mizaj?

In Unani medicine every person has a temperament, or mizaj: a balance of hot and cold, dry and moist. Answer twelve questions about your natural tendencies, not how you feel today. It takes about two minutes.

    What this quiz can and cannot tell you

    The questions are adapted from the classical "ten signs" (ajnas-e-ashra) that Unani practitioners use, such as build, response to heat and cold, sleep and emotional style. Your answers are scored on two axes, hot to cold and dry to moist, and plotted on the same map we use for herbs.

    Temperament is a traditional framework, not a validated medical test. It cannot diagnose disease, and modern research has not shown that temperament predicts health outcomes. We do not store your answers.
