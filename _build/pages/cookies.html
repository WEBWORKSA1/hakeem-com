What we store ourselves

Hakeem.com stores two small preferences in your browser's local storage: your light or dark theme choice, and your answer to the cookie notice. They never leave your device.

Advertising cookies

Google AdSense, our advertising partner, uses cookies and similar technologies to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance and, where you allow it, personalise them. Google may also use these cookies for fraud prevention. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see a consent message before personalised advertising cookies are used.

Embedded content

YouTube videos are embedded in privacy-enhanced mode and load only after you press play. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Your choices

  • Choose "Essential only" in our cookie notice to record your preference.
  • Manage personalised ads at Google Ads Settings.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site works without them, though ads may be less relevant.

More detail is in our privacy policy.
