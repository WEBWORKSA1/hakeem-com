A note on using herbs for health problems

Herbs can be a reasonable first step for mild, familiar complaints such as occasional constipation or an upset stomach. They are a poor substitute for diagnosis when symptoms are new, severe or persistent. If you take prescription medicines, check our interaction checker and speak to your pharmacist before adding anything.
