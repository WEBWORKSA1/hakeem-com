Old remedies, honest evidence.

Hakeem.com checks the herbs of Unani, Ayurveda and other healing traditions against modern research, so you can see what works, what is unproven, and what is unsafe with your medicines.

Popular: Kalonji Ashwagandha Haldi Isabgol Mulethi

Every profile is graded on a five-level evidence scale and cites the US National Institutes of Health, Memorial Sloan Kettering, the European Medicines Agency or peer-reviewed reviews.
