About Hakeem.com

In Urdu and Arabic, a hakeem is a wise person, and in South Asia the word means a practitioner of Unani medicine. We take that ideal of wisdom seriously: respect for a thousand years of tradition, and honesty about what modern research shows.

Why we exist

Hundreds of millions of people use herbal remedies, often alongside prescription medicines and often on the advice of relatives, shopkeepers or social media. The reliable information that exists is scattered across government fact sheets, pharmacopoeias and journals that most people never see, and very little of it speaks to the Unani and South Asian remedies that families actually use.

Hakeem.com brings that information together in one place, in plain language, with the names people actually see on labels: kalonji as well as Nigella sativa, mulethi as well as licorice root.

What we do

  • Herb profiles that separate traditional use from tested evidence, grade each use on a five-level scale, and put safety and interactions near the top.
  • Tools such as the interaction checker and the mizaj quiz.
  • Guides to Unani theory, safe buying and how to judge a health claim.
  • Introductions to qualified practitioners for readers who want personal advice.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell herbs or supplements.
  • We do not claim that any herb cures disease.
  • We do not let advertisers or sponsors influence content.

Who writes and reviews

Content is written and checked by the Hakeem.com Editorial Team against the sources cited on each page: the US National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health, Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center's About Herbs database, the National Library of Medicine's LiverTox, the European Medicines Agency and peer-reviewed reviews. We are recruiting licensed pharmacists and physicians to form a medical review board; their names and credentials will appear on each page they review.

How we are funded

Hakeem.com is free to read. It is funded by advertising served by Google AdSense, practitioner directory subscriptions, labelled sponsorships and reader support. Read our editorial and advertising policy.
