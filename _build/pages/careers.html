Work with us

We are a small, remote team building the most trustworthy guide to herbal and traditional medicine on the web. Most roles are freelance or part-time to start, paid per piece or per hour.

  • Medical reviewer (pharmacist or physician)

    Review herb profiles for accuracy, safety and interactions. Current licence required. Remote, paid per profile.

  • Unani content writer (BUMS or MD Unani)

    Write traditional-use sections and guides from classical sources, with references. Urdu reading ability essential.

  • Ayurveda content writer (BAMS)

    Write Ayurvedic sections and comparison guides. Sanskrit terminology and Hindi a plus.

  • Research editor

    Find and summarise clinical trials and reviews, keep evidence grades current. Background in pharmacy, nutrition or public health.

  • Translators: Urdu, Hindi and Arabic

    Translate herb profiles and guides, keeping medical meaning exact. Paid per word.

  • Video producer

    Turn herb profiles into short explainers for YouTube and social channels, in English, Urdu or Hindi.

  • Community and social media manager

    Run contests, moderate stories and grow our newsletter and social channels.

  • Partnerships lead

    Recruit practitioners and clinics to the directory and manage sponsors. Commission-based, with experience in healthcare or wellness sales.

  • Volunteer

    Proofreading, plant photography and local knowledge. Volunteers are credited on the site.

How we work

  • Fully remote, with written briefs and clear deadlines.
  • Paid on time, by bank transfer, PayPal or UPI.
  • Every piece is credited to its writer and reviewer once our review board launches.
  • Evidence first: we would rather say "we do not know" than overstate a claim.

Hiring is partly funded by reader support. Support the team.
