Use of the word "hakeem"

"Hakeem" (also spelled hakim, حکیم or حكيم) is a common Arabic, Persian and Urdu word meaning a wise or learned person, and in South Asia a practitioner of Unani medicine. It is also a widely used personal name. Hakeem.com uses the word only in this generic, descriptive sense, as the domain name of an educational website about herbal and traditional medicine.
